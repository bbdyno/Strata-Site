Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Short version: Strata collects no data. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not communicate with any server. In-app purchases are handled by Apple.

Scope

This policy covers the Strata app for Mac, distributed through the Mac App Store, and this website. Questions about privacy can be sent to della.kimko@gmail.com.

What Strata reads on your Mac

Strata runs in the App Store sandbox and can read only what you allow. When you first open it, you choose Macintosh HD to give it read access; if you also turn on Full Disk Access, it can measure locations that macOS otherwise keeps private.

To measure storage, Strata reads the names, sizes and dates of files and folders, and information about disk volumes. It does not read the contents of your files.

What stays on your Mac

Scan results and your cleanup history are stored only on your Mac, inside Strata’s own app container. They are never uploaded, and we have no access to them.

What we collect

Nothing. Strata does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data or usage data. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, third-party SDKs or crash-reporting code, and it does not connect to our servers or any other server.

If you have chosen in macOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics under Apple’s own privacy policy. This is controlled by you and by Apple, not by Strata.

Purchases

The in-app purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. Strata uses Apple’s StoreKit to check whether the purchase has been made. Apple handles your payment under Apple’s privacy policy; we do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and keep the service secure; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The site uses no cookies, analytics or advertising of its own.

Email

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and delete the conversation when it is no longer needed.

Children

Strata collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because the app collects nothing, we hold no app data about you. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to show you or delete that correspondence by writing to della.kimko@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
