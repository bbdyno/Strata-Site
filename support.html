Frequently asked questions

How do I give Strata permission to read my disk?

Mac App Store apps can only read the locations you choose. The first time you open Strata, it shows Allow Strata to Read Your Disk:

  1. Click Choose Macintosh HD…. The window that opens already starts at the top of your disk, Macintosh HD.
  2. Leave it there and click Allow.

Strata remembers this permission. If you choose a folder inside Macintosh HD instead, or cancel the window, Strata asks again.

Do I need to turn on Full Disk Access?

No, it is optional. Even after you choose Macintosh HD, macOS keeps some locations private, such as Mail, Messages, Safari data and parts of your Library. Strata marks what it could not read as “not measured”, so you always know how complete the picture is.

To include those locations:

  1. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Full Disk Access.
  2. Turn on Strata. If it isn’t in the list, click the add button (+) and choose Strata from your Applications folder.
  3. Quit and reopen Strata.

You can turn it off again at any time.

Why does Strata ask me to copy a command?

App Store apps run in a sandbox and cannot run other programs. Some caches can only be removed properly by the tool that made them, such as Homebrew, Xcode simulator runtimes or the Android SDK. In the App Store version these items can’t be checked for cleaning. Select the item, click the copy button next to its command in the inspector on the right, paste it into Terminal and press Return.

Copying commands is free.

Can I undo a cleanup?

Yes, as long as you haven’t emptied the Trash. Strata moves the files it cleans to the Trash. To restore something, open the Trash, select the item and choose File › Put Back.

Commands you run yourself in Terminal are carried out by that tool. Caches regenerate on their own, and runtimes or system images can be downloaded again.

What is free, and what does the purchase add?

Downloading Strata is free. Scanning, the disk map, every explanation and copying cleanup commands are free, and your first cleanup is free too.

After that, Strata shows Cleaning needs Strata Pro. Click Buy Strata Pro to unlock cleaning in full with a one-time in-app purchase. It is not a subscription. The price is shown in the App Store.

How do I restore my purchase?

On a new Mac or after reinstalling Strata:

  1. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you used to buy.
  2. Open Strata. On the purchase screen (Cleaning needs Strata Pro), or in Settings › Purchase, click Restore Purchases.

Family Sharing follows Apple’s rules. If you share purchases with your family, family members can restore the purchase the same way on their own Macs.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple and we cannot issue them ourselves. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account, choose “Request a refund” and select your Strata purchase. Apple decides on refund requests under its own policy.

How does Strata get updated?

Through the Mac App Store, like any other App Store app. Strata itself never downloads anything.

Does Strata collect any data?

No. There are no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and Strata does not talk to any server. Scan results and cleanup history stay on your Mac. See the privacy policy.

Does Strata read the contents of my files?

Only when you start Find Duplicates yourself. To tell whether two files are identical, Strata compares their contents on this Mac and sends nothing anywhere. Every other scan reads only file information such as names, sizes and dates.

How do I uninstall Strata?

Quit Strata and move it from the Applications folder to the Trash. If you turned on Full Disk Access, you can also remove Strata from that list in System Settings.
